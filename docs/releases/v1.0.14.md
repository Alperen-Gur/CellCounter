# CellCounter v1.0.14 for macOS

This patch fixes further Review Queue failures reported in [issue #10](https://github.com/Alperen-Gur/Cellographer/issues/10), building on the large-library improvements in v1.0.13.

Requires **macOS 15 or later**. Universal app for Intel and Apple Silicon, ad-hoc signed and not notarized. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/Alperen-Gur/Cellographer/blob/v1.0.14/docs/INSTALL.md).

## Review and save reliability

- Repair stale review entries left behind by older deletion or reset behavior, while preserving live detections and corrections.
- Guard background work against records deleted during decoding, editing or reanalysis. Serialize image and contour decoding to limit overlapping work.
- Save review decisions and undo before advancing the queue. Show read, decoding, encoding and save failures.
- Finish pending Results edits before review decisions, and prevent delayed edits from overwriting a newer analysis.
- Recount pending candidates after changes so delayed notifications cannot restore an outdated review badge.
- Refresh review measurements after recalibration and defer review decisions until calibration updates finish.
- Handle invalid measurements and submicron diameter-edit ranges safely.

## Preview reliability

- Recover missing or damaged thumbnails from a bounded preview of the imported image.
- Decode thumbnails before rendering, with a maximum image size.
- Keep the raw pixel orientation used by detections when displaying review previews.
- Keep thumbnails at least one pixel wide and high, including very thin images.
